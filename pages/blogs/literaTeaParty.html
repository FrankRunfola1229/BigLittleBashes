Litera-TEA Party!


You are invited to Caleb and Adalina's Litera-Tea Party!

This party is not your cookie-cutter (no pun intended!), traditional tea-party. We swapped the pearls, vintage tea-set, muted pastels and literary classics for bright, vibrant pops of color and our centerpiece is modern day childrens' books that speak to the important issues of our world today.

As parents of a multi-racial/multi-cultural family, I know my husband and I struggle with how to approach and engage our children in discussions on race, religion, gender and other kinds of activism. When's the right time to bring it up? How DO you bring it up? Is there a right way? Our oldest is beginning to ask questions and has been picking up on how he may not look like many of his friends. For our daughter, I hope that her value in life goes beyond her appearance (no matter how cute!) and she is recognized for the clever little mind she has.

I guess what we're saying is that there may be no 'perfect time' or 'perfect age' to get started. Instead, we threw them a Litera-TEA party on our deck outside, ate some delicious cookies from The Blooming Flour bakery and enjoyed an afternoon of reading and illumination. It was a delicious, sweet day of learning and joy for the Runfola kids.

Are you looking to throw a traditional or not-so-traditional tea party for your little (or big!) ones? We're up for it all.
