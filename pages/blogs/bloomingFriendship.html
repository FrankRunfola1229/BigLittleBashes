The Blooming Friendship

In addition to being one of my best friends for nearly a decade, Kristina and I have seen each other through it all. The latest addition to our friendship is our common desire to follow our hearts in opening up our respective small businesses. Kristina owns The Blooming Flour bakery, and is my family's go to for custom cookies, cakes and cupcakes.

She's done special event confectionaries for pretty much every milestone in my life from my bridal and baby showers to my wedding and kids' birthdays. It only made sense that her artistry and baked goods would make their way to Big Little Bashes' Preferred Partners list.

On the day of our collab shoot for the kids' Litera-Tea party, as an Event Curator (and technically client) I couldn't be more grateful to work with a baker who truly buys into my vision. She was able to decorate the Spring Solstice cookies in a way that complimented the event design palette. They were absolutely delicious and I had a hard time not putting my iPhone camera down to devour them myself. During our concept work phase, she presented many ideas and was always keen and open to feedback.

I was especially delighted when she baked her custom shaker cookies, that are her bakery's specialty. The Shaker Cookies are a delight to all your senses! They are gorgeous, with a clear middle filled with sprinkles or a custom design of your choice. You can shake them and see the sprinkles and glitter move inside, and the best part of it- it's all edible! The kids were truly in for a treat with those.

I know a lot of professionals don't recommend mixing business with pleasure, but working with Kristina has and (I know) will always continue to be a positive and rewarding experience for me. We truly get each other, and that makes both our jobs easier. She's always been someone who puts other's before herself and pleases everyone, and I'm glad that she finally decided to prioritize her own dreams of opening her bakery and putting herself out there. She's certainly inspired me to do the same for myself. The Litera-tea party was the event that has now planted the seeds to a blooming friendship and future project collaborations!

While you may all see her as this fancy, awesome baker on her socials, she will always be that girl who befriended me on my first day in a big company, making me feel warm, welcome and always well-fed. Love you, my sweet friend.
