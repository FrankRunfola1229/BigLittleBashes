Our Story

Hi there, I'm Rohini Runfola and I am the Owner and Lead Event Curator for Big Little Bashes. I am an MBA graduate from the University at Buffalo, and have 8+ years experience working as a Vice President in the world's largest bank in Risk Management and Data Analytics. Prior to my career in Finance, I worked in Advertising and Digital Marketing in Telecommunications, and hold a certification in Art Marketing from the Sotheby's Institute of Arts. I'm very lucky to have been married to my best friend, Frank, for 6 years and together, we have 2 devious little toddlers, Caleb and Adalina, who are our world and then some!

My Inspiration

My love for event planning and design has its roots in my childhood. My fondest memories growing up have been theunique and thoughtful birthdays my parents planned for me. I recall every year having a special theme- complete with custom cuisine, decorations, games and favors. Every personalized aspect of these gatherings was a physical representation of the love and joy in our little family. That sentiment is something I hold dear to my heart, andtry to bring to all the celebrations that I have been a part of, such as my own wedding and children's birthdays, since then.

My Values

As your Event Curator, I don't rely solely on Pinterest posts or Social media trends; I like to understand 'you' or who you are celebrating. We're going to spend a good chunk of time getting to know each other, as this helps me truly get a feel of what you're looking for and how I can curate your event to make your celebrant feel like the main event! I like to plan games, create special decor or make custom favors that incorporate a sentimental element that leaves guests feeling both entertained and connected emotionally with both the host and each other. In our Mood Board Proposals, I am happy to include any specific vision or ideas you have. If there is a very specific design concept or theme that you've previously seen executed before, I'm happy to replicate, but going the extra mile with thoughtful personalization is where Big Little Bashes shines the best! I'm always happy to leverage our network of vendors in the business that can help carry out your ideas, as well!

My Process

My background in Data Modeling and Analytics helps me with my organization and planning skills. I like to joke that I plan my entire life in Excel spreadsheets, which is really quite true! I track my work pipelines and expenses via Event/ Project Management software, that creates transparency for my clients and keeps me on-task and also, very importantly, on-budget. As a working mother, time is my most precious resource, which is why I prefer to pick and choose my projects, and like to only take on a certain number of events on my work-slate at a time. This way, I can ensure that as my client, you receive my full attention and prompt on-going communication. Lack of or delayed communication are some of my biggest pet peeves when working with vendors, so rest assured I'm always up for a quick chat or text if you need me.

My Niche

My absolute favorite events to plan are small, intimate gatherings such as birthday parties (kid and adult) and other such milestone celebrations. These events really give me a chance to flex my creative skills to create an amazing experience for guests and hosts alike. Also, as a nod to my Indian heritage, I love to plan culturally diverse events such as Indian weddings, pujas, festivals and ritualistic traditions. Having planned my own wedding to an Italian-American, I especially enjoy the thought of planning mixed, bi-racial and multicultural weddings.

My committment to you

Through your planning process, you can consider me your emotional support party-animal! Planning large events are generally considered stressful and overwhelming experiences, but my main goal would be to always make sure you get what you want and to advocate for your wishes. Conflicting opinions and theatrics amongst friends? Intrusive and over-stepping family members, who really might mean well? I'm happy to step in to take that stress from you and bear the brunt of 'the bad guy' if you will. My prior experience in managing teams and working in a large, politically charged corporation has armed me with a toolkit of effective communication and interpersonal skills that I am not shy to use when situations arise. I am so humbled that you've made it this far in reading Our Story. I'm so excited to work with you, and be a part of your joy. At the end of the day, OUR Story is really, all about YOU and bringing YOUR story to life in all the Big, Little ways that we can!
