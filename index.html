Welcome to Big Little Bashes!


Exclusive Party Design and Premier Indian Wedding Planner in Buffalo, NY. We specialize in curating exclusive celebrations that are sentimental and intimate, regardless of scale or occasion. Whether it's a birthday party, grand wedding or cultural ceremony, we work hard to imbibe YOUR personal touch to the affair.

We pride ourselves in executing unique and creative concepts and themes, and especially enjoy bringing multi-cultural and out-of-the-box concepts to life in our event design and collateral. Currently our planning packages include Birthdays, Weddings, Showers and Ceremonial functions/rituals. We are happy to customize the suite of services provided to work within your vision and expectations.

Our growing network in the party planning industry is one of our strongest assets. While we are happy to provide thoughtful recommendations on vendors, we are always willing to partner with new ones to deliver your ideas. Thank you for taking the time to take a look at our website! Please feel free to reach out if you have any questions. We look forward to celebrating all your Big Little moments!
